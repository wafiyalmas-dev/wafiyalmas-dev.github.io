About me

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Nanotechnology Engineering and work at the point where materials science meets energy. My research covers hydrogen production, hydrometallurgy, lithium-ion battery recycling, catalyst materials and semiconductor thin films for solar cells.

In the lab I plan experiments, characterize materials (XRD, SEM-EDS, XRF, XPS, ICP-MS, BET), analyze the data and write the technical report. Outside the lab I have led teams: I chaired Airlangga Nanotechnology Week 3.0 and headed the Scientific Department of the student association.

I’m open to roles in Engineering, R&D, Renewable Energy, Advanced Materials, Battery Technology, Laboratory Analysis and Process Development.
