About me

I’m a fresh Nanotechnology Engineering graduate (S.T., GPA 3.52/4.00) with hands-on research experience in advanced materials and energy technology. My work covers Li-ion battery recycling through hydrometallurgy and pyrometallurgy, hydrogen generation and measurement, and semiconductor nanomaterials for solar cells, and I’m especially interested in precious metal refinery.

I plan experiments, characterize materials (XRD, XPS, SEM-EDS, XRF, ICP-MS, BET), analyze the data and document the results.
